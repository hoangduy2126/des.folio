01_ABOUT_ME

HOÀNG DUY

Randommoee06 / GRAPHIC DESIGNER / HANOI, VN 🇻🇳

Hi, I'm Hoàng Duy (Randommoee06). I'm a graphic designer and design student based in Hanoi, Vietnam. To me, design has never been just about making things look cool—it's about finding honest ways to tell stories, solve problems, and connect with people.

Whether building a visual identity from scratch, crafting social media campaigns, or exploring typography and brutalist aesthetics, I put care and intention into every detail. I'm constantly learning, staying curious, and looking for meaningful collaborations with people who care about what they build.
